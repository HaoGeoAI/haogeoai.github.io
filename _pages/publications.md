## Research Topics Represented in Current Manuscripts

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Vision–Language Models for Ecological Interpretation</h3>
  <p>My current work explores how vision–language models can extend wildlife monitoring beyond object detection by supporting structured ecological outputs, including species identification, instance counting, behavior classification, metadata extraction, visual question answering, and ecological reasoning.</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Thermal Remote Sensing and Drone-Based Wildlife Monitoring</h3>
  <p>This research investigates how thermal drone imagery can be combined with lightweight multimodal adaptation strategies to improve wildlife species recognition and instance counting in conservation landscapes.</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Multi-Perspective Wildlife Monitoring</h3>
  <p>This research integrates drone-based aerial observations and ground-based camera trap imagery to support spatial analysis of wildlife distributions and human–wildlife interactions.</p>
</div>
