## Selected Projects

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Swimming Pool Detection for Property Tax Assessment</h3>
  <p><strong>City of Southlake, TX · 2024</strong></p>

  <p>Fine-tuned Faster R-CNN using pretrained deep learning models in ArcGIS Pro on high-resolution aerial imagery, achieving 98% detection accuracy for residential swimming pool identification to support municipal tax reporting.</p>

  <p>Applied spatial interpolation and georeferencing with Google Earth data to generate 10 cm resolution imagery. Compared results against NAIP imagery at 60 cm resolution, demonstrating parcel-level precision gains for property feature detection.</p>

  <p>Compared the performance of multiple fine-tuned deep learning model configurations to identify the optimal detection pipeline.</p>

  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> Faster R-CNN · ArcGIS Pro Deep Learning · High-resolution aerial imagery · Georeferencing · Spatial interpolation</p>
  <p><strong>Applications:</strong> Property tax assessment · Municipal GIS · Parcel-level feature detection</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Airbnb New York City — Geospatial Market Intelligence &amp; Geocoding</h3>
  <p><strong>2024</strong></p>

  <p>Geocoded and standardized Airbnb listing data for New York City using longitude and latitude coordinates. Built dot density maps and descriptive statistics to identify spatial patterns across neighborhoods.</p>

  <p>Applied Moran's I and LISA cluster analysis in GeoDa to identify statistically significant hot spots and cold spots of Airbnb listing activity.</p>

  <p>Conducted buffer analysis on candidate hotel sites using Business Analyst Online to assess market viability, including dining density, employment centers, and commute-time accessibility.</p>

  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> Geocoding · Dot density mapping · Moran's I · LISA cluster analysis · Buffer analysis · Business Analyst Online</p>
  <p><strong>Applications:</strong> Market intelligence · Hotel site selection · Urban spatial analysis</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Tweet Sentiment Classification — Distributed NLP Pipeline</h3>
  <p><strong>2025</strong></p>

  <p>Developed an end-to-end distributed natural language processing pipeline using PySpark on Databricks for tweet sentiment classification.</p>

  <p>Implemented TF-IDF feature extraction, logistic regression modeling, and hyperparameter tuning with CrossValidator to classify tweets into positive, neutral, and negative sentiment categories.</p>

  <p>Achieved F1 score greater than 0.82 and accuracy greater than 0.85 across the three sentiment classes.</p>

  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> PySpark · Databricks · TF-IDF · Logistic Regression · CrossValidator · NLP classification</p>
  <p><strong>Applications:</strong> Sentiment analysis · Distributed machine learning · Social media analytics</p>
</div>
