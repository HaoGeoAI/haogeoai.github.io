# Research

My research integrates GeoAI, remote sensing, computer vision, and spatial analysis to support environmental monitoring and conservation applications.

## Research Areas

### GeoAI and Environmental Remote Sensing

I apply geospatial artificial intelligence and remote sensing technologies to analyze environmental systems and conservation landscapes.

### Wildlife Monitoring with Drones and Camera Traps

I use drone thermal imagery and camera trap data to support automated wildlife detection, species recognition, instance counting, and spatial analysis.

### Vision–Language Models for Ecological Interpretation

I develop VLM-based approaches for structured ecological interpretation, including species identification, counting, behavior classification, metadata extraction, and ecological reasoning.

### Spatial and Spatiotemporal Analysis

I use KDE, IDW, daily presence records, landscape stratification, and temporal proximity metrics to analyze wildlife distributions and human–wildlife interactions.
