My research integrates GeoAI, remote sensing, computer vision, and spatial analysis to support environmental monitoring and conservation applications.

## Research Interests

- GeoAI and environmental remote sensing
- Wildlife monitoring using drones and camera traps
- Vision–language models for ecological interpretation
- Spatial and spatiotemporal analysis of human–wildlife interactions
