## About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Geospatial Information Sciences at The University of Texas at Dallas. My research integrates GeoAI, remote sensing, computer vision, and spatial analysis to support environmental monitoring and conservation applications.

My current work focuses on AI-driven wildlife monitoring using drone thermal imagery and camera trap data. I develop deep learning and vision–language model frameworks for species recognition, instance counting, ecological attribute extraction, and spatial analysis of human–wildlife interactions.

## Selected Research Projects

### CameraTrap-Instruct: Vision–Language Modeling for Camera Trap Imagery

CameraTrap-Instruct is a vision–language framework for structured ecological interpretation of camera trap imagery. The framework supports species identification, instance counting, behavior classification, OCR-based metadata extraction, visual question answering, and ecological reasoning.

**Methods:** Vision–language models, LoRA, multimodal projector alignment, camera trap image analysis  
**Applications:** Wildlife monitoring, biodiversity assessment, ecological interpretation  
**Status:** Manuscript in preparation

---

### Thermal Vision–Language Model Adaptation for Drone-Based Wildlife Monitoring

This project investigates how RGB-pretrained vision–language models can be adapted to thermal drone imagery for wildlife species recognition and instance counting through lightweight multimodal projector alignment and instruction tuning.

**Methods:** Thermal remote sensing, VLM adaptation, LoRA, drone imagery, species counting  
**Applications:** Aerial wildlife monitoring, conservation remote sensing, ecological AI  
**Status:** Manuscript in preparation

---

### Multi-Perspective Wildlife Monitoring Using Drones and Camera Traps

This project integrates drone thermal imagery and camera trap observations to map wildlife distributions and assess human–wildlife interactions across conservation landscapes.

**Methods:** YOLO detection, drone thermal imagery, camera traps, KDE, IDW, spatial analysis  
**Applications:** Conservation planning, human–wildlife interaction assessment, environmental remote sensing  
**Status:** Manuscript in preparation
